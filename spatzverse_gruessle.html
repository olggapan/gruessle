A local joke.
A tiny game.
A whole universe.

Walk through an exaggerated version of Ulm. Meet residents, tourists and a very judgmental little sparrow. Choose your replies, build your Schwaben-Respekt and discover how different people react.

01 / THE HOOK
Short, relatable local interactions produce funny unexpected scores and reactions. Built for 20-second screen-recorded Reels.
02 / THE GAME LOOP
Approach NPC → hear or read their line → pick one of 3 responses → instant reward, surprise and combo → next street.
03 / THE LOCAL TWIST
Münsterplatz → Fischerviertel → Donaubrücke. A recurring Ulmer Spatz narrator, Kehrwoch humor, regional words and a self-aware Bayern border joke.
